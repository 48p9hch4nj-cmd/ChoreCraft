 FIELD GUIDE

Build a maze. Hold the core.

Choose a weapon, tap a tile to preview, then confirm. Walls and towers redirect ground enemies. Drones fly over them.

Batch. Between waves, turn Batch on. Choose Place to tap several new defenses onto the board, Upgrade to tap several towers for one level each, or Remove to mark several structures. Tap a red removal mark again to keep that structure. Confirm applies the batch; Cancel restores everything. Existing structures return 70% when removed; cancelling a new unconfirmed placement returns its full cost. Waves wait until you confirm or cancel.

Tactics. EMP slows enemies and disables shields for 5 seconds ($2,000). Overdrive doubles weapon fire rate for 6 seconds ($4,000). Each can be used once per wave. Mark enemy is free: tap a threat and every weapon that can hit it within range prioritizes it. Clear the mark to return to normal aiming. Pause holds ability timers too.

Four weapons. Arc sentry cleans up light ground and air targets, chaining to a second enemy and bypassing shields. Siege mortar blasts ground crowds. Rail battery is a 2×2 armor-piercing ground weapon for tanks and bosses. Skyguard is dedicated anti-air.

2×2 hubs. Cryo hub slows ground enemies in its blue field by 35%. Power relay makes guns in its gold field fire 25% faster. Each upgrade improves the field effect by 10 percentage points and slightly increases range. Fields of the same kind do not stack. All four footprint tiles must be clear; tap any part to select, remove or upgrade the whole machine.

Upgrades. Tap a tower for its Upgrade button. Steel, blue and gold mounts show its three levels. Its range ring stays visible while selected; brackets mark its priority target.

Aiming. Guns fire automatically at enemies they can hit within range. Closest to core stops escapes: leave most guns on this. Most health wears down tougher enemies: try it on a Railgun against tanks or bosses, with a Pulse or Gatling on Closest to core nearby. You can change either choice during combat for free.

Shields. From wave 7, shield carriers have a blue ring. They and nearby ground troops take 35% less damage. Arc sentries bypass shields; EMP turns them off for 5 seconds.

Controls. Two fingers move and zoom the map. Move mode lets you drag with one finger. Build mode keeps it steady. A temporary line previews your proposed route; no purchase is needed. Swipe sideways through weapons.

Armored enemies. Armor reduces ordinary weapon damage. Rail batteries and mortars pierce armor. Cryo hubs slow ground enemies; Arc sentries chain to two targets.

Waves. Launch once. Auto-play sends following waves after five seconds. You can build during combat. Pause also stops the countdown. Selling returns 70% of the money invested.

Battery. Battery saver defaults on for phones: 30 FPS, fewer decorative effects, and no redraws while paused or idle. Combat timing stays the same. Reduced effects separately removes extra particles.

Your preparation phase is saved in this browser. An interrupted wave restarts from its beginning when you resume.
